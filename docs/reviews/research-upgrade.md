### Creative development that changes the work

Input: the [fictional workshop brief](../../examples/brands/harbor-repair/brand.md), which supplies routine tune-ups and puncture repair but no verified location, turnaround, prices, photos, or destination.

| Direction | Concept brief | Proof and production decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation clarity | Explain what to include when requesting help for a puncture before a planned commute | Useful text guide; no promise about same-day service or commute readiness |
| Demonstration | Show what a routine-maintenance appointment covers | Requires actual owner service details and genuine media; text checklist can precede media |
| Alternative tradeoff | Explain when to describe the problem to a workshop rather than assume a particular repair | Needs accurate scope; no invented superiority over DIY or other workshops |

These differ in situation/proof mechanism, rather than merely changing the hook. Refine a factual baseline: “Request a puncture repair or routine tune-up. Include what is happening with your bike and the service you need.” It remains an unpublished fixture draft; a real destination, availability, accurate service details, and owner review are unresolved. No generated customer outcome or synthetic vote chooses the winner. Relevant procedures: [creative development](../research/creative-development.md), [concept evaluation](../../prompts/concept-evaluation.md); T57–59, T62–63.

## Review closure and remaining evidence

The marketing-method reviewer found no material gaps in the revised research/creative requirements, procedures, and scenarios. The evaluation/startup reviewer found four narrower inconsistencies. The primary editorial closure corrected them and reread the affected requirements, procedures, record templates, architecture, and cases; no additional independent sign-off is claimed.

| Final review finding | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Order swaps applied only to “important” pairs | Every decision-influencing pair initiates both orders; unavailable reversal stays provisional; T60 checks initiation |
| Fixture and outcome calibration were blurred | Separate defect detection from audience prediction; record task/domain/reference data and development/evaluation separation; T62 adds a required variant |
| Effort limits existed only in acceptance prose | Declare and track pass/call/time limits before judging in procedure, prompt, template, PRD, and domain record |
| T63 could pass through one of several alternatives | Three mandatory parameterized cases independently check factual blocking, unavailable critic fallback, and cap exhaustion |

Every requirement is represented in the acceptance suite; code fences and local link targets resolve. Workspace text/path scan found no excluded reference terms, personal home paths, named real-business defaults, or inherited private brand content. These checks cover the current workspace, not a future repository history or release archive.

Review findings are analytical judgments; no universal effectiveness or reliability claim follows. Only document checks and the labeled manual walkthrough were completed. All runtime and live-channel checks remain pending.
