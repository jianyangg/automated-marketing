# Research consolidation log

Date: October 13, 2026. Question: which existing research can improve a reusable, Codex-operated marketing workflow without participant recruitment or a separate model service?

## Scope and selection

Include primary research on secondary-review methods, public customer language, creative development, context, measurement, and model critique. Prefer studies with inspectable designs/outcomes and explicit limitations. Include favorable and unfavorable simulation findings. Exclude product advertising as effectiveness evidence, repeated summaries as independent studies, inaccessible details, and techniques requiring new participant data. Existing published participant studies remain eligible sources.

The [evidence library](evidence-library.md) records the 15 selected sources, inspected access, findings, and transfer limits. Initial exploration covered those topic families; the exact earlier query sequence is not reconstructed here. This retained log records the final consolidation and version audit, not an exhaustive systematic search history.

## Synthesis and stopping decision

Adopt transparent research runs, source-linked corpus coding, distinct creative premises, separate novelty/relevance checks, placement-specific reasoning, bias-aware model critique, and honest outcome interpretation. Exact prompts, rubrics, record fields, and review counts remain design choices. Each transfer maps to PRD requirements and acceptance scenarios.

Stop this bounded upgrade once these mechanisms and their counterevidence are documented and independently challenged. Remaining gaps include the workflow's actual business value, new-user usability, implementation reliability, current-model task calibration, cross-category transfer, and replication strength. These gaps remain open; additional citations alone cannot close them.
