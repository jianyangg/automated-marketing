# Build a complete campaign

## Inputs

Adopted or explicitly provisional goal/strategy, selected opportunity, proof, resources, destination, and available capabilities.

## Procedure

1. Write the campaign brief: objective, audience/situation, problem, proposition, proof, offer/core asset, destination, channel rationale, sequence, dependencies, resources, observation window, and exit criteria.
   Consult relevant past campaign results and `reports/learning.md` first; inspect their source evidence and context. Record what is reused, changed, avoided, or retested. File the brief and subsequent work under `work/campaigns/<date>-<slug>/` with a stable ID and index link, following the brand workspace contract. Do not create loose root-level campaign documents.
2. Check product/service availability, material claims, proof rights, and destination readiness. Turn gaps into tasks or blockers; never invent supporting evidence.
3. Follow `docs/research/creative-development.md`. When comparing approaches, develop materially different premises/proof mechanisms before polishing one. Check originality and customer/product relevance separately; record why alternatives were retained or rejected. Then create the core asset and intentional adaptations: guide, service/product demo, conversion change, email/lifecycle concept, FAQ, or social content according to the diagnosis.
4. Preserve source/context/version lineage for every variant. Include usable copy, assets or clear asset requests, alt text, links, scripts/instructions, and expected next action.
5. Use existing permitted tools and text/template fallbacks. Do not buy generation, require a second LLM, or claim unavailable integrations work.
6. Use `prompts/concept-evaluation.md` if model critique helps, and review the pack through `prompts/review.md` before presenting it as ready. Record reader intent, placement, interruption and next-action burden; sharing potential and qualified conversion remain different outcomes.
7. Prepare exact manual handoff or request actual scoped execution authority. Prepared work, authorization, local schedule, and remote publication remain distinct.
   Preserve material versions and source/derived links. Update the brand index and record result status, even when unlaunched or awaiting measurement; do not invent execution or outcomes. Put later observations/decisions in the campaign's `results.md` through the learning workflow.

## Deliverables

Campaign brief, core asset, complete variants/change packs, dependency/measurement plan, review findings, and exact handoff. Outward-facing copy is self-contained in the chosen brand voice; internal sources, reasoning, and approval notes stay separate.

## Review

Challenge audience/channel fit, excessive deliverables, weak proof, generic claims, broken destination, owner production burden, and whether one useful adaptation would outperform a larger batch.
