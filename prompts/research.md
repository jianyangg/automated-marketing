# Conduct autonomous secondary research

Read `docs/research/secondary-research.md` for the full method and `docs/research/evidence-library.md` for reusable evidence. Use this workflow when external evidence could materially change a marketing decision; do not wait for the owner to provide papers.

1. Save the decision question, context, inclusion rules, query families, effort cap, and potential disconfirming evidence.
2. Actively use available web search/open tools. Separate published technique evidence from current business/category evidence. Prefer primary scholarly sources for effectiveness and official sources for product/platform facts.
3. Retrieve and inspect the actual material, following public author/institutional copies if needed. Record access level; do not claim a full-paper read from a snippet/abstract. Never fabricate citations or bypass access controls.
4. Search contrary/null/boundary evidence. Deduplicate study and publication lineage. Check study task, design, outcomes, population, timing, and applicability before adopting a method.
5. When public customer language is useful, collect traceable positive/negative/mixed/substitute-satisfied rows under a declared corpus plan. Keep direct statements and coding interpretations separate. No personal profiling or synthetic padding.
6. Synthesize bounded finding → adaptation → mechanism → limitation → acceptance check. Label research findings, hypotheses, source facts, and model critique separately.
7. Save the search/evidence/decision record and next review triggers. Record what customers said as `signal add` (with its source link) so bets can cite it. If evidence remains inadequate, return a narrow test or a conversation with real users, not a confident guess.

Real users are the strongest evidence: the owner's conversations, support messages, product behavior, and a short fit survey once there are 40+ active users. Use their words only with consent and without identifying details. No synthetic consumer panels, invented personas or simulated polls. Model critique follows `prompts/concept-evaluation.md` and never substitutes for human outcome measurements.

Output: concise decision, best supporting and contrary evidence, applicability limits, recommended method/action, and linked private research artifacts. Paid services, private-account access, recurring jobs, and external writes need their own actual authority; ordinary public web research within the requested task does not need another permission question.
