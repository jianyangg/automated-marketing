# Learn from outcomes and choose the next action

## Inputs

Campaign/validation hypothesis, expected metric/window, actual observations, delivery records, operator effort/cost, and known data limits.

## Procedure

1. Confirm what actually ran and which version, account, destination, action, and time apply. Prepared/scheduled work is not a result.
2. Preserve source definitions, report digests, units, windows, correction history, and denominator. Reimports must not double-count. Delayed/suppressed/unavailable data is not zero.
3. Separate editorial feedback, operational quality, and market response. Owner approval is not acquisition; clicks are not installs; correlation is not incremental revenue.
4. Compare genuinely comparable observations. Explain small samples, internal traffic, attribution limits, and absent instrumentation.
   Classify conclusions as descriptive, attributed, associational, or causally identified. Tagged conversions do not establish incrementality. Causal claims need an existing credible design and its assumptions; identify seasonality, simultaneous changes, and low power. No significant result is not proof of no effect. Do not require new surveys or participant experiments.
5. Revisit the bottleneck and alternatives. A result can suggest destination work, a clearer test, channel change, more evidence, or waiting rather than more content.
6. Record continue/change/stop/wait, evidence, uncertainty, the affected strategy/work, owner resource implication, and next review/action.
   Save campaign observations and the decision in its `results.md`; link source reports and exact artifact versions. Distinguish cancelled/unlaunched/awaiting-measurement from measured success or failure.
7. Update the compact operating brief. Preserve past records and hold incompatible future work when strategy changes.
   Revisit the evidence/method record when sources are corrected, settings change, or observed outcomes contradict the earlier transfer. Successful defect detection only calibrates that fixture task; it does not calibrate audience prediction.
8. Curate reusable findings in `reports/learning.md`: source campaign/task and versions, context, observation versus hypothesis, confidence/limits, proposed reuse/change, and review trigger. Retain contrary evidence and link superseded lessons. Update `index.md` and material decision links so a new session can retrieve the result without the chat. Use section 10 of `docs/brand-workspaces.md`; do not duplicate full reports into the lesson index or generalize a one-off result into a universal rule.

## Deliverables

A short evidence-backed decision and next action, with a linked reproducible detail record. Never declare a business winner from engagement alone or hide incomplete data to keep automation moving.
