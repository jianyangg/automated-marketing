# Review work before execution

## Pass 1: business and creative challenge

Check goal, customer situation, alternatives, proposition, proof, channel/destination fit, owner effort, and whether the selected action addresses the bottleneck. Look for a plausible failure and counterevidence. Check that old marketing was evaluated rather than inherited and that synthetic evidence remains labeled.

Check source applicability and selection bias, originality versus relevance, conceptual duplication, and placement/CTA burden. If LLM comparison influenced the decision, inspect rubric, order sensitivity, shared-model bias, and unresolved disagreement. An internal preference cannot establish human preference or predicted lift. No new participant research is required to pass review.

If a decision framework was used, check whether it changed the recommendation, exposed a tradeoff, or identified disconfirming evidence. Check coherence of audience/promise/capabilities and source support for journey/competitive claims. Remove unsupported scores and redundant framework reports; practitioner authority is not empirical validation.

## Pass 2: truth and operating challenge

Check material claims/current availability, source and asset rights, actual copy/render, link/account, typed action, target resources/recipients, timing, cost, measurement, and implemented capability. Verify that the artifact version being reviewed is the one the grant/handoff will identify.

For installed automation, check current local dependency digests/fresh inventory, remote-source freshness, trusted authority, notice, worker enrollment, and unresolved attempts. A valid session/CSRF token alone is not owner provenance. A copied or stale workspace cannot silently resume execution.

## Findings and correction

Record only actionable findings with affected artifacts and consequence. Correct material findings, then recheck the affected contract. Preserve versions and owner edits. Do not mark unresolved blockers as passed.

## Optional specialist critique

Use separate customer/strategy, creative/truth, or measurement/reliability review only when delegation is authorized and useful. Provide bounded context and a review question. Reconcile disagreements into one decision; reviewers cannot authorize spending/publication or start competing queues.

## Authority result

Return ready for owner review, ready within existing scoped authority, or blocked with a concrete next action. Do not ask again for an action already concretely authorized. Do not turn silence, a proposal, or an agent-generated approval field into permission.
