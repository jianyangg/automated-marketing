# Evidence card

- Evidence ID / reviewed date:
- Title, authors, year, DOI / exact URL:
- Version/status: peer-reviewed, working paper, correction/retraction status if relevant:
- Access inspected: full text, methods/results section, abstract only, or inaccessible lead:
- Question, task, population/category/channel/model/date:
- Design/data/sample actually inspected:
- Outcome measured and bounded finding:
- Limitations, counterevidence, overlap with other source lineages:
- Applicability to the present decision:
- Proposed adaptation and expected mechanism:
- Acceptance check / local calibration evidence needed:
- Distinguish published finding, analyst inference, and untested implementation choice:
- Review/expiry triggers and affected strategy/artifacts:

Do not turn a general study effect, a vendor claim, or an LLM score into a campaign forecast. Missing method details remain unknown.
