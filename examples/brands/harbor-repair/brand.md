# Harbor Repair

Fictional business used to test the framework. Every business fact below is synthetic fixture data, not externally verified research.

## Offer

A neighborhood bicycle repair workshop offering routine tune-ups, puncture repairs, and appointment-based maintenance. It serves commuters within a short ride of its workshop. Appointments are requested through a booking form; no website or live account is supplied in this fixture.

## Desired outcome

More qualified maintenance appointment requests. Current request count, conversion rate, customer mix, pricing, opening hours, and available appointment capacity are unknown.

## Constraints

- Draft-only example. No accounts, spending, jobs, or publication authority.
- No invented reviews, customer results, opening hours, discounts, prices, or repair guarantees.
- Do not imply electrical bicycle servicing or emergency callouts are offered.
- Use a practical, friendly voice and one clear next step.

## Assets

No photos or logo are supplied. A useful text-led draft and a precise photo request are acceptable. Generated images must not claim to show the actual workshop or staff.

## Initial research questions

Who is most likely to request routine maintenance, what prevents booking, and does the booking destination explain the service well enough? These are questions to investigate, not validated audience findings.
